## About Me

I'm a Chicago-based software developer and IT program manager who turns real-world needs into clear, reliable digital products. My work spans **full-stack development, business technology, web strategy, and creative production**, with a particular focus on helping small businesses and organizations strengthen how they operate and connect with people online.

I combine hands-on engineering with practical project leadership—organizing complex work, setting priorities, and carrying ideas from concept through delivery. I'm driven by useful outcomes, thoughtful user experiences, and the belief that knowledge becomes more valuable when it is shared.

## Core Focus

- **Full-Stack Engineering:** Responsive interfaces, backend systems, databases, integrations, and maintainable application architecture.
- **Technology Leadership:** Requirements, planning, prioritization, stakeholder communication, and dependable delivery.
- **Small-Business Solutions:** Websites, digital workflows, online visibility, and technology that solves practical business problems.
- **Creative Development:** Visual design, motion, video, and interactive experiences that give products a distinct identity.
